About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a lightweight note-taking app for capturing ideas before they disappear. It keeps your short notes, reminders, and lists in one simple place without distractions.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Type your note into the input field.
  2. Click "Add Note" to save it.
  3. Find your saved notes in the "Your notes" list.

Features

  • Add notes instantly from the home page
  • See a live count of your notes
  • Simple, distraction-free layout
  • Works in any modern browser

Keyboard shortcuts
